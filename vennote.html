HOW IT WORKS END-TO-END

THE PARTNER WORKFLOW

STEP 01

APPLY & GET PROMO CODE

Submit your branches & coverage radius below. Once approved, you appear in the app Matchmaker and receive your unique VAR Promo Code.

STEP 02

RECEIVE FARM BLUEPRINT

When a farmer selects you (or unlocks with your Promo Code), you get an email with their satellite blueprint, notes, and a direct Installer Portal link.

STEP 03

MOUNT & SUBMIT DEVEUIS

Install the gateway and field nodes, drag pins in your portal to confirm exact GPS, enter the DevEUIs & LNS choice, and hit Submit to HQ.

STEP 04

EARN EVERY MONTH

StilSein handles app provisioning, Jakkals AI topography, and monthly SaaS billing and pays your recurring commission every month.

Who handles software support & billing?

StilSein HQ handles app support, cloud uptime, and monthly SaaS invoicing. You only handle physical hardware calls if a sensor is damaged in the field.

Can I serve multiple towns or provinces?

Yes! Add as many branches as you like in the application form below, each with its own HQ pin, 50–1000km radius, and supported sensor services.

What if a farmer only wants Soil or Security?

Rain, Pipe, and Dam modules are live today. You can pre-register for GrondWolf (Soil) and WagWolf (Security) so you're automatically matched as they launch.
