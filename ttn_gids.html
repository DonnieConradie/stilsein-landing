THE THINGS NETWORK (TTN) INTEGRATION

TTN WEBHOOK GUIDE

Connect your LoRaWAN sensors from The Things Stack (TTN) or a private LNS directly to the StilSein Edge Telemetry Router in minutes.

1

Open Webhooks in TTN Console

Log into your The Things Stack (TTN) Console, select your Application, open Integrations → Webhooks on the left-hand menu, and click + Add webhook.

2

Select the StilSein Industrial Tile

On the Webhook Templates grid, select the StilSein Industrial tile. The base URL (https://api.stilsein.co.za), JSON format, and uplink path are pre-configured automatically.

Webhook ID: stilsein-edge-router
StilSein Security Token: Paste the secret token provided in your StilSein onboarding confirmation email.
3

Create Webhook & Verify Telemetry

Click Create stilsein webhook. Your LoRaWAN sensors will immediately stream encrypted uplinks into your StilSein dashboard. Ensure each sensor's DevEUI in TTN matches the hardware registry in your WeerWolf app.

1

Access Your Webhooks

Log into your TTN / Private LNS Console and navigate to Applications → Webhooks → + Add webhook.

2

Choose Custom Webhook

If using a private LNS or setting up manually prior to template availability, click the Custom webhook tile.

3

Configure the Cloudflare Edge Bridge

  • Webhook ID: stilsein-edge-router
  • Webhook Format: JSON
  • Custom Header: X-StilSein-Auth (your StilSein Security Token)
  • Enabled Event Types: Tick Uplink message only.
https://api.stilsein.co.za
4

Save and Ignite

Click Add webhook at the bottom of the page. Your hardware will begin streaming encrypted telemetry to StilSein immediately.
