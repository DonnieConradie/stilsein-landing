The Things Network (TTN) Webhook Guide
Welcome to the StilSein ecosystem! To connect your existing hardware to our Jakkals AI matrix, we need to create a secure bridge between your TTN Console and our Cloudflare Edge Routers.

1 Access Your Webhooks

Log into your TTN Console and navigate to your specific Application. Click on Applications (Step 01), select Webhooks on the left-hand menu (Step 02), and click the blue + Add webhook button (Step 03) as indicated by the arrows below.

2 Choose Custom Webhook

On the template screen, ignore the pre-configured tiles and click directly on the Custom webhook tile at the top.

3 Configure the Cloudflare Bridge

Fill in the configuration exactly as indicated by the arrows below:

  • Arrow 1 (Webhook ID): Type stilsein-edge-router
  • Arrow 2 (Webhook Format): Select JSON
  • Arrow 3 (Base URL): Paste the secure link below exactly as it appears.
https://api.stilsein.co.za

Finally, under Enabled event types (Arrow 4), ensure that the checkbox for Uplink message is ticked. Leave all other fields blank.

4 Save and Ignite

Click the blue Add webhook button at the bottom of the page. Your hardware will begin streaming encrypted telemetry to our servers immediately. Our engineering team is currently verifying your topography, and you will receive an email containing your unique Farm Key within 24 hours.

If you experience any issues, please contact our engineering team at admin@stilsein.co.za.
