PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

StilSein Industrial (Pty) Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") operates a B2B Industrial IoT (Internet of Things) platform specifically designed for the African agricultural sector. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the StilSein mobile application, website, and associated hardware.

2. Telemetry & Agricultural Data

StilSein's primary function is the collection of non-personal environmental telemetry (e.g., rainfall, pipe pressure, dam levels, soil moisture). This hardware data is routed securely into isolated, farm-specific cloud vaults.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your farm's telemetry data to third parties, marketers, or insurance companies without your explicit consent.

3. Personal Information Collected

To provision your account and facilitate hardware installation, we collect:

  • Name and Surname
  • Contact Number and Email Address
  • Farm Name and Agricultural Operation Type

This information is used strictly for account management, billing, and sending automated system alerts (e.g., Weekly PDF Reports).

4. Location Data (GPS)

The StilSein app utilizes your device's location services only when the app is actively in use (Foreground). This is used exclusively to:

  • Display your physical distance from your farm's Gateway and sensors on the Interactive Radar Map.
  • Drop an accurate GPS pin during the "Farm Builder" setup wizard.

We do not track your location in the background, nor do we store your mobile device's live location history.

5. Artificial Intelligence (Jakkals AI)

Our platform utilizes "Jakkals AI," powered by Google Gemini, to analyze your telemetry. When you request an analysis, your farm's anonymized telemetry for the selected timeframe is sent to the AI model to generate agricultural insights. Google does not use this data to train their public foundation models.

6. Data Security

We implement bank-grade encryption for data in transit (via The Things Network and Cloudflare Edge Proxies). Each client's data is siloed in a dedicated, private database vault to prevent cross-tenant data leakage.
