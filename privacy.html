PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

StilSein Industries (Pty) Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") operates a B2B Industrial IoT (Internet of Things) platform specifically designed for the African agricultural sector. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the StilSein mobile application, website, and associated hardware.

2. Telemetry & Agricultural Data

StilSein's primary function is the collection of non-personal environmental telemetry (e.g., rainfall, pipe pressure, dam levels, soil moisture). This hardware data is ingested via encrypted LoRaWAN gateways, routed through Cloudflare Edge security proxies, and stored within a dedicated PostgreSQL database cluster protected by strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies to prevent unauthorized cross-tenant access.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your farm's telemetry data to third parties, marketers, or insurance companies without your explicit consent.

3. Personal Information Collected

To provision your account and facilitate hardware installation, we collect:

  • Name and Surname
  • Contact Number and Email Address
  • Farm Name and Agricultural Operation Type

This information is used strictly for account management, billing, and sending automated system alerts (e.g., Weekly PDF Reports).

4. Location Data (GPS)

The StilSein app utilizes your mobile device's location services strictly when the application is open and actively in use (Foreground Only). This permission is never accessed in the background and is utilized exclusively to:

  • Calculate your real-time physical distance from the central LoRaWAN Gateway and field sensors on the Interactive Farm Radar.
  • Enable precision GPS pin placement when designing new infrastructure within the interactive Farm Builder wizard.

StilSein does not monitor, record, share, or store historical user movement or background location logs.

5. Artificial Intelligence (Jakkals AI)

Our platform utilizes "Jakkals AI," powered by Google Gemini, to analyze your telemetry. When you request an analysis, your farm's anonymized telemetry for the selected timeframe is sent to the AI model to generate agricultural insights. Google does not use this data to train their public foundation models.

6. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We implement bank-grade encryption standards for all telemetry in transit and at rest. Sensor payloads are encrypted over the air using LoRaWAN AES-128 cryptographic keys, ingested via TLS 1.3 encrypted endpoints on Cloudflare Workers, and persisted in an enterprise-grade PostgreSQL database cluster hosted on Supabase.

Tenant isolation is enforced cryptographically and relationally at the database engine level via PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS). Public client queries are strictly blocked, ensuring that each farming enterprise's telemetry remains completely unreachable by other accounts.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your farm's telemetry and account records for the duration of your active SaaS service agreement to maintain continuous 5-year micro-climate historical models and regulatory compliance.

In compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play policies, users maintain the absolute right to delete their account and all associated telemetry. You may request immediate, permanent eradication of your account, nodes, and historical data at any time by:

  • Using the in-app account deletion function under Wolf Cave > Settings.
  • Emailing our data compliance team at dev@stilsein.co.za with your Farm Key.

Upon verification, all personal identifiers, farm topology, and telemetry logs will be permanently expunged from our database clusters within 30 days.

8. Regulatory Compliance & Inquiries

StilSein Industries (Pty) Ltd operates in full alignment with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and international data protection standards (GDPR).
