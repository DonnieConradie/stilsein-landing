STILSEIN USER MANUAL

Welcome to the control center. Learn how to navigate your digital farm, interpret advanced telemetry, and configure your Jakkals AI alerts.

The Command Central of your farm. A unified 3x3 grid that gives you an instant overview of your entire operation.

The Global Top-Dock

  • The Pulse Dot: Indicates connection health. Blue = Fresh (< 15 mins). Yellow = Delayed. Orange = Stale. Red = Offline.
  • Module Dropdown: Filter the grid to show only Rain, Pipes, Dams, or Forecasts.
  • Timechip Dropdown: Shift the entire dashboard back in time (Live, 24H, 7D, 30D, 1Y).

Hardware & Software Cards

  • Physical Nodes: Tiles displaying live animations (Rain cylinders, Pipe flows, Dam waves).
  • Virtual Nodes (Average/Overview): Tiles showing the mathematical average of an entire network.
  • Add Node: Tapping an empty transparent tile allows you to instantly request a quote to expand your hardware network.

Your farm rendered in geospatial reality. Tap the Gateway node in the center of the Omni-Dashboard, or the Map icon in the bottom taskbar to enter.

Navigation & Tools

  • Filtering: Expand the bottom dock menu to toggle specific hardware types (Rain, Pipe, Dam) on and off.
  • Live Location (Blue Dot): Toggle 'Position' to see your physical GPS location on the farm, including your exact distance (km) to the Gateway.
  • Center Focus: Tap the crosshair icon to instantly zoom out and fit the entire farm boundary back on the screen.

Interacting with Nodes

  • Signal Paths: Dashed lines indicate active radio paths. If a node is raining, the path thickens and pulses rapidly.
  • The Floating Stage: Tapping any node marker on the map blurs the background and opens that specific sensor's Live Stage directly over the map for quick analysis.

The deep-dive view. Accessed by selecting a specific module from the top dropdown, or tapping a specific node on the Map or Omni-Dashboard.

The Data Cockpit

  • Status Banner: A dynamic pulse indicator below the main value. Green = STABLE/HEALTHY. Red = LEAK/OVERPRESSURE/OFFLINE. Orange = LOW BATT.
  • Timers (Top Right): Shows exactly when the next data pulse is arriving. It shifts to "LISTENING..." and "LATE" if interference occurs.
  • Health Stats: Signal strength (dBm) and Battery % are always visible flanking the status banner.

Interactive Elements

  • Mini-Charts: Tap the small chart at the bottom of the card to open the Fullscreen Analytical God-Mode.
  • Expand/Collapse: Use the arrows on the right to reveal the comparative grid of all other nodes in that category.
  • Swipe Navigation: Swipe the main cylinder left or right to seamlessly switch between nodes in the field.

The analytical God-Mode. Designed for deep dives into historical telemetry, leak investigations, and PDF report generation.

Navigation & Analysis

  • Sticky Y-Axis: When zooming or panning horizontally through time, the measurement scale (left) stays securely pinned to the screen.
  • Double-Tap to Isolate: For dual-charts (e.g., Pipe Line Chart + Gantt Status Track), double-tapping either chart expands it to full-screen mode for easier viewing.
  • Interactive HUD: Tap any point on a line or bar to reveal the exact Date, Time, Value, and specific hazard warnings (e.g., 🔴 OVERPRESSURE).

Action Bar

  • Jakkals AI Paw: Tap the orange paw icon to instantly send the currently viewed chart data to Jakkals for an expert Agricultural analysis.
  • Unit Toggle: Tap the '%' or 'kL' icon to switch the mathematical rendering of Dam levels on the fly.
  • PDF/CSV Export: Tap the download arrow to instantly generate a branded PDF or raw Excel spreadsheet of the chart.

Hyper-local meteorological predictions based on your farm's exact GPS coordinates. Accessed from the top-left dropdown menu.

The 7-Day Overview

  • The Grid: A high-level view of the upcoming week. Days with expected rainfall are highlighted with a glowing blue border.
  • Quick Navigation: Tap on any specific day in the grid to instantly jump to its detailed triple-chart view.

The Triple-Chart View

  • Temperature (Top): The color gradient shifts from Cold (Blue) to Perfect (Green) to Heatwave Threshold (Red).
  • Precipitation (Middle): Hour-by-hour bar charts showing exact expected accumulation.
  • Wind Profile (Bottom): Blue-to-Lavender gradients indicate wind speeds, peaking on gale-force days.

Your farm's permanent historical ledger. Every critical event is safely locked away here for future reference.

The Ticker-Tape Inbox

  • Unread Events: Events you haven't viewed yet will pulse with an orange border and a "NEW" tag.
  • Event Types: Displays Rain Spells (Total mm), Pipe Incidents (Min/Max Bar), Dam Incidents (Rapid Drains), and System Logs (Green microchip icon).
  • Interacting: Tap any event in the list to instantly open its high-resolution, isolated fullscreen chart.

Data Management

  • PDF / CSV Export: Tap the download arrow icon on any event to export a professional, branded PDF or an Excel-ready CSV file.
  • Loading History: Scroll to the bottom of the list and tap "LOAD PREVIOUS WEEK" to pull older archives from Deep Freeze storage.

The IT Dashboard. Manage your app settings, push notifications, and monitor global hardware health.

Settings & Notifications

  • Alarms: Turn specific module notifications on/off. Use the "Snooze" button to silence all alarms for 8 hours.
  • Preferences: Toggle Haptic Feedback (vibrations), switch between Dark and Light mode, and change the app language.

Hardware Diagnostics

  • Status Dots: Check the Active Sensors list. Green = Healthy, Orange = Low Battery, Red = Offline.
  • Health Charts: Double-tap the Battery or Signal (RSSI) line charts to open fullscreen, 24-hour diagnostic views.
  • Clear Cache: If you suspect your app data is stuck, tap "Clear Cache & Sync" to force a fresh download from the Vault.

Every Sunday night at midnight, Jakkals complies your entire week's telemetry into a beautiful PDF and CSV ledger.

The Sunday Night Protocol

  • Automated Delivery: You don't need to do anything. The report is automatically emailed to you, ensuring your farm's data is safely backed up and ready for Monday morning.
  • AI Summaries: The report includes a witty, professional "Looking Back" summary of the past week, and a "Looking Forward" summary of the upcoming week's weather.

Manual Exports

  • Export Tool: You can manually generate PDF or CSV exports at any time by tapping the Export icon in the bottom taskbar, allowing you to select specific nodes and timeframes.
