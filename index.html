FIELD DEPLOYMENT & TELEMETRY

HOW YOUR FARM CONNECTS

Forget weak cell signal in distant camps. Battery-powered sensors beam data up to 15km via LoRaWAN® radio to your homestead Gateway, and our Farm Builder makes arranging hardware & installation effortless.

🗺️
1. PIN IN FARM BUILDER

Open the free StilSein app and drop pins on your satellite farm map where you want Rain, Pipe, or Dam sensors. Choose DIY or pick a certified local installer near your farm.

📡
2. SENSORS & GATEWAY

A compact Gateway at your farmhouse catches radio signals from battery sensors (up to 5-year battery life, 15km range) with zero SIM cards needed out in the veld.

🔗
EXISTING HARDWARE OR PRIVATE LNS?

Already running Dragino, Milesight, SenseCAP, Netvox, NCD, or Browan nodes on The Things Network (TTN) or ChirpStack? You don't need new hardware. Just enter your DevEUIs in the app, point your Webhook to our Edge router, and your sensors go live in StilSein.

☁️
3. EDGE CLOUD & AI

Incoming hex packets are decoded at the Cloudflare Edge, evaluated against your pipe & dam thresholds, stored in your isolated database vault, and analyzed by Jakkals AI.

📱
4. PHONE & DESKTOP APP

Your farm unlocks with a unique Farm Key. View live gauges, satellite radar maps, offline-cached history, and receive instant alerts on iOS, Android, and Web Desktop.
